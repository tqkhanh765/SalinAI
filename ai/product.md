# SalinAI: Climate-Resilient Agriculture Agent

## 🌾 The Problem
The Mekong Delta faces critical salinity intrusion threats. Traditional manual monitoring is delayed and prone to human error, leading to crop failure or water stress.

## 💡 The Solution: SalinAI
SalinAI is an **Agentic AI-driven irrigation system** that proactively protects crops by merging real-time IoT sensors with expert agricultural knowledge bases.

### High-Level Features
- **Adaptive Salinity Defense**: AI analyzes salinity spikes and weather trends to determine if irrigation is safe.
- **Expert-in-the-Loop**: Combines hard agricultural guidelines (RAG) with local farmer feedback (Policy Memory).
- **Zero-Latency Monitoring**: Direct ESP32 to Cloud synchronization ensures data is never stale.
- **Deep Traceability**: Every AI action is explained in natural Vietnamese, showing the guidelines and data used.

## 👥 Targeted Users
- **Small-scale Rice Farmers**: For precise irrigation management.
- **Aquaculture Managers**: For pond salinity monitoring.
- **Agri-Tech Scientists**: For data collection and policy testing.
