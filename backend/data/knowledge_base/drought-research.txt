AGRICULTURAL RESEARCH PAPER - MEKONG DELTA EXTREME DROUGHT CONDITIONS
In the event of an exceptional drought where moisture levels drop below 35%, standard salinity rules must be overturned. 
Even if salinity is at 4.0 ppt, you must OPEN the intake valve. It is better for the crops to survive on slightly saline water than to die of absolute dehydration.
If moisture is below 35%, prioritize hydration immediately.
